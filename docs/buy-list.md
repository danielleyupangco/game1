# The buy list

*Every item, tagged so the list can be worked rather than read. Source data: a
friend's 2022 registry (`CJ`), plus a short set of additions of our own
(`Added`) where that list had a gap. The verdict column is CJ's own hindsight,
recorded after she had actually used the thing — `must`, `skip`, or `note` where
she left a comment worth reading.*

**Every peso figure is 2022 and every link is a 2022 link.** Prices have moved
and product pages rot; the brand and the item name are the durable part, which
is why the page builds a fresh store search for every row regardless of whether
CJ's original link survives.

**Priority is CJ's**, from her own High / Medium / Low column — what she judged
a first-wave purchase against what could wait. It is not a judgement about
anyone else's baby.

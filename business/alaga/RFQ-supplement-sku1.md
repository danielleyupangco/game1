# RFQ — ALAGA FLEX (SKU #1, dog joint soft chew)

Send this to every supplier on the target list. Do not shorten it. The length is
deliberate: it signals a serious buyer, and questions 6–10 are the ones that
separate a factory that can actually get you registered in the Philippines from
one that will waste six months of your time.

**Subject line:** `Private label RFQ — dog joint soft chew, Philippines import, recurring monthly volume`

---

## Message body

Hello,

I am launching a private-label pet supplement brand for the Philippine market and
am shortlisting manufacturing partners for our first SKU. We expect to place a
first order within 60 days of selecting a partner, followed by recurring monthly
replenishment.

**About us**
We are a Manila-based company building a premium dog supplement brand sold direct
to consumers online and wholesale through grooming salons and pet specialty
retail. First SKU is a joint-health soft chew. A skin and coat SKU follows once
the first is registered.

**Product specification we are seeking**

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Format | Soft chew, poultry or beef flavour, high palatability |
| Target actives per chew | Glucosamine HCl ~500 mg, Chondroitin sulfate ~400 mg, MSM ~250 mg, plus Omega-3 (EPA/DHA), turmeric/curcumin, hyaluronic acid, Vitamin C and E |
| Pack sizes | 60-count primary; quote 120-count as an option |
| Packaging | Quote both HDPE bottle and foil stand-up pouch, each with desiccant |
| Shelf life | Minimum 18 months, validated in hot and humid conditions |
| Labelling | Fully private label, our artwork, our brand name |

**Please quote and confirm the following. Numbered replies are appreciated.**

1. **FOB price per unit** at 1,000 / 2,500 / 5,000 / 10,000 units, for 60-count and
   120-count, and your named FOB port.
2. **Minimum order quantity** for a first private-label run, and whether you will
   accept a smaller trial run.
3. **Lead times** — sample dispatch, artwork-to-production, and full production.
4. **Sample policy** — cost and timeline for 3–5 units for palatability and
   stability evaluation.
5. **Tooling, plate, or setup charges**, if any, and whether they are refundable
   against volume.

**Regulatory documentation — these decide our selection**

6. Can you supply a **Certificate of Free Sale** issued by the competent authority
   in your country of origin for this product? Please state the issuing body.
7. Can you supply your facility's **GMP certificate**, and confirm whether the
   facility is **FDA-registered** and/or **NASC-certified** (or the equivalent in
   your jurisdiction)?
8. Can you supply the **complete product composition and formulation** in the
   detail required for a government registration dossier, including specifications
   for each active?
9. Do you hold **stability study data for hot and humid climate conditions**
   (ICH Climatic Zone IVb — 30°C / 75% relative humidity)? If not, can you commission
   it, and at what cost and timeline? *This is a hard requirement — the Philippine
   Bureau of Animal Industry requires stability data valid for our climate, and soft
   chews are the format most prone to failing it.*
10. Can you supply **pharmacological and toxicological data** and a
    **Certificate of Analysis** per batch?
11. Have you previously exported to the **Philippines or Southeast Asia**? If so,
    can you say which markets, and whether the importer completed registration
    successfully?

**Commercial terms**

12. Payment terms for a first order and for repeat orders.
13. Will you grant **exclusive or priority distribution rights for the Philippines**
    against an agreed annual volume commitment? Please state the volume that would
    be required.
14. Your capacity for **monthly replenishment** once we are established.

We are evaluating suppliers in both the United States and Asia. Documentation
completeness carries as much weight as unit price in our decision, because it
determines whether we can register the product at all.

Thank you — I look forward to your reply.

## Scoring the replies

Score each supplier 0–2 on each line. Anything scoring 0 on rows marked ★ is
disqualified regardless of price — no dossier, no registration, no business.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Certificate of Free Sale | Cannot supply | Can obtain, unclear timeline | Holds it / routine to issue |
| ★ GMP certificate | None | Claimed, no document | Certificate provided, facility named |
| ★ Zone IVb stability data | Cannot provide or commission | Can commission, costly/slow | Already holds tropical stability data |
| Full formulation disclosure | Refuses | Partial | Complete, registration-ready |
| FOB price | Above ₱600 landed | ₱450–600 landed | Below ₱450 landed |
| MOQ | Above ₱300,000 outlay | ₱150,000–300,000 | Below ₱150,000 |
| SEA / PH export track record | None | Region, not PH | Successful PH registration |
| PH exclusivity offered | Refuses | Negotiable | Offered at reachable volume |
| Sample speed | Over 4 weeks | 2–4 weeks | Under 2 weeks |

**Decision rule:** take the highest total score, not the lowest price. A supplier
who is ₱80 per unit cheaper but cannot produce a Certificate of Free Sale costs you
the entire registration path and the year that goes with it.
